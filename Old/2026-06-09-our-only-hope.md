## Introducing Hope [3 mins]
• Intro – today we are discussing what Christians call our hope.
• "Hope" = a steady, joyful confidence about the future.

## Discussion question: what are the key ingredients for hope?

## Jesus is our hope
• Recap: sin --> seperation --> death/judgement.
• Jesus came to save sinners.
• Jesus dealed with the effects of sin.
• More than that; he dealed with sin by taking on its effects on himself.
• Betrayed; false trial; humiliated; flogged; crucified...
• Read gospel extract: Luke 23:
• Things to notice: 1) what the criminal admits; 2) why the criminal is saved
• Jesus didn't stay dead, but rose on the third day.
• What this means for me: everything changes.
• God is real; God loves me; I need God.
• I can have forgiveness and life beyond death. I can have hope.

## Discuss: what do you think of Jesus? What do you find compelling; what do you find confusing?

## Interview with Peter. [5 mins].

• Story of a house; Father and Son; son runs away from father and does all sorts of things that bring shame on the father; son messages Father and says I don't deserve to come back; give me some sign that you want me back; paint the letterbox bright blue. Early next morning goes and finds that not just the letterbox, but the whole house is painted blue. His dad standing out the front.

• Our sin seperates us from God; but Jesus' death and resurrection is this big, clear sign that God wants us back forever.

• Finish with Romans 6:23.
