## Introductory question: how do you make big decisions in life?
• Fast and impulsive vs. slow and deliberate
• What are the big questions you ask first?
• Can you give examples?

## Who or what will I live for?
• Story of humanity = lots of different ways of making the decision.

• Recap: Over the last few weeks we've seen that Jesus really died and really rose. Tonight we're asking a different question—not 'Did it happen?' but 'If it did, what should we do about it?'

• Jesus died to pay the penalty for our sin; and he rose from death to life to prove his divinity and win a great victory.
• During his life, Jesus called people to follow him, but as he did that he also helped people weigh the decisions.
• Luke 9:25 – what good...
• Luke 14:28 – suppose you want to build a tower...
- changing priorities
- giving up control
- trusting him over yourself
- following him even when culture disagrees
- putting him before career, comfort or reputation
+ purpose - becoming the person you were made to be
+ forgiveness
+ knowing God
+ eternal life
+ hope beyond death

## Discussion: What would it look like for you to weigh up following Jesus? (Or what did it look like)

• Whatever barriers exist, they are worth pushing through if Jesus is real.
• Story of Paul in Athens to highlight: --give context -- Acts 17:24–31 -- explain what happened next.

• Future concern: justice, judgement for those who have ignore God; life for those who repent.

• Notice: many "believe". What does it mean to become a disciple of Jesus? Follow him... trust in him.
• Trust ≠ certainity, moral improvement first
• Trust = relational reliance on Jesus who died and rose again
• Trust = leads to living the way Jesus taught; i.e. how to live for God.

• You don't have to have every question answered tonight.
• But you shouldn't ignore Jesus either.
• If he really rose from the dead, then he deserves to be taken seriously.
• So keep exploring, ask your questions, and if you've become convinced that Jesus is Lord, don't wait forever before following him.
